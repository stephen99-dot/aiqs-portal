# AI QS 1.1.0 (build 3) — App Store submission notes

Everything below is ready to paste into App Store Connect. The build itself
comes from the Mac: `git pull`, `npm run ios:sync`, `npx cap open ios`, then
Product → Archive → Distribute (docs/IOS_APP.md §8). The version and build
number are already set in the project (1.1.0, build 3).

## What's New (App Store, customer-facing)

```
Scan a room, survey it, price it — all from your phone.

• Room surveys: walk a property room by room. Photos, sizes, doors and
  windows, fittings, finishes, condition and notes are kept with each room,
  and the quantities a quote needs are worked out for you.
• LiDAR scanning on iPhone Pro: measure a room in seconds. The plan draws
  itself, squared up with doors and windows where the scanner saw them, and
  you can turn it round in 3D.
• Show the finished room: paint the work the customer wants onto a photo —
  new tiles, a new bath, the walls done — and compare before and after.
• Talk to MAX: dictate what the customer wants or a site note using your
  iPhone's own speech recognition. MAX replies in writing.
• Survey report: a branded PDF with the plan, quantities, photos and the
  finished-room concept, plus an Excel schedule.
• Fixes and polish throughout.
```

## App Review notes (the "Notes" box on the version page)

```
Thank you for reviewing AI QS.

Sign in with the demo account below. It is a builder's account with MAX
(the AI estimator) switched on and a sample property survey already
created, so every feature can be tried without waiting for anything.

  Email:    [demo account email]
  Password: [demo account password]

Where things are:
• Menu → Scan a room: the property survey. Open "Demo house" → a room to see
  photos, the plan and 3D view, quantities and the actions.
• "Show the finished room" (top of the Photos step) sends a photo and the
  builder's description of the work to an AI image service and shows the
  result as a before/after. The result is labelled as an AI concept, not to
  scale, in the app and in the PDF report.
• "Scan with LiDAR" appears only on iPhone 12 Pro or later / iPad Pro (it
  uses Apple's RoomPlan). On other devices the app offers "Import a scan"
  and photos instead, so nothing is broken on a non-LiDAR device.
• Ask MAX (menu or the bottom bar): the AI assistant. The microphone uses
  Apple's on-device speech recognition; MAX answers in text only.

Permissions: camera (photos of the room and LiDAR scanning), microphone and
speech recognition (dictating to MAX and site notes), photo library (adding
existing photos). Audio is turned into text and is not stored.

Purchases: nothing is sold inside the app. Plans (the MAX packages) and
credits are managed in the customer's AI QS account on the website. The app
shows what is on the account and never shows a price or a link to buy.

Quantity take-offs and BOQs for submitted drawings are produced by our QS
team within 24 hours, so a drawings submission made during review will not
return a document immediately; the demo account already contains a finished
one to look at.
```

## Before you press Submit — checklist

- [ ] Demo account: create (or reuse) a client account with MAX on
      (Admin → Users → the account → MAX → "Give early access to MAX") AND
      the Visualiser on ("Give the Visualiser free" on the same card — without
      it "Show the finished room" shows a locked notice), a survey with two
      rooms, a photo in each, a scope of work, one finished-room render, and
      one completed drawings project. Fill its email and password into the
      notes above.
- [ ] Render allowance: Admin → MAX → finished-room renders per month is
      above zero, and OPENAI_API_KEY is set on Render (it is, if renders work
      on the website).
- [ ] App Privacy answers in App Store Connect match the manifest: photos /
      user content, email, name, phone, device token — linked to the account,
      not used for tracking. Nothing new is collected in this release; audio
      is transcribed on the device and not kept.
- [ ] Screenshots: at least the 6.7" set (iPhone 15 Pro Max / 16 Pro Max).
      Worth including: the room page with the plan, the LiDAR scan, the
      before/after slider, MAX with a priced estimate, the survey report.
- [ ] Privacy policy URL still valid.
- [ ] TestFlight first: install build 3 on your own phone from TestFlight and
      run through scan → survey → render → price once before submitting.
